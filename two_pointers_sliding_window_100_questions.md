# Two Pointers & Sliding Window: 100-Question Practice Sheet

A structured practice list for DSA interviews and competitive
programming. Problems are grouped by pattern and roughly ordered from
beginner to advanced.

**How to use this sheet** - Solve in order within each section; try each
problem before checking editorial hints. - Write down the invariant
(what your pointers/window represents) before coding. - Record time and
space complexity after every solution. - Suggested language: C++17. -
Difficulty labels are approximate and may vary by platform.

# Pattern Checklist

Before calling a problem solved, confirm:

-   [ ] I can explain why the left/right pointer or window moves.
-   [ ] I know the invariant maintained after each iteration.
-   [ ] I handled duplicates, empty inputs, and boundary cases.
-   [ ] I can derive the time and space complexity.
-   [ ] I can solve it again without looking at the previous code.
